Title: Synthetic CO2 hydrogenation test rig and technical route (demo only)

1. Apparatus
1.1 The rig starts from a gas manifold (A1) that supplies the CO2/H2 feed.
1.2 A mass flow controller (A2) sets the total feed to 50 mL/min.
1.3 The two streams are combined in a static mixer (A3).
1.4 The mixed gas passes through a preheater (A4).
1.5 The preheated feed enters the fixed-bed reactor (A5).
1.6 The effluent is cooled in a condenser (A6).
1.7 The gas phase is routed to a GC analyzer (A7) for analysis.
1.8 The condensed liquid is drained to a vent line (A8) as waste.

2. Working conditions
2.1 The manifold outlet (A1.out) feeds the controller inlet (A2.in).
2.2 The preheater is held at 180 °C.
2.3 The reactor is held at 350 °C and 1 bar with GHSV = 12 000 h^-1.
2.4 The gas chromatograph reports CO2 conversion and CH4 selectivity.

3. Technical route
3.1 S1 Define target; S2 Prepare catalyst.
3.2 D1 asks whether CO2 conversion is at least 80% at 350 °C after 12 h.
    If yes, continue to S3 Stability test 100 h; if no, go to S2b Reformulate
    catalyst and return to S2.
3.3 S3 leads to T1 Report and scale-up.

4. Ports and media
K1 A1.out -> A2.in, medium CO2/H2
K2 A2.out -> A3.in, medium mixed gas
K3 A3.out -> A4.in, medium mixed gas
K4 A4.out -> A5.in, medium preheated feed
K5 A5.out -> A6.in, medium effluent
K6 A6.gas_out -> A7.inlet, medium gas sample
K7 A6.liquid_out -> A8.drain, medium condensate (waste)
