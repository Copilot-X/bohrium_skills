- **Target (en):** Surface oxygen vacancies are proposed to regulate the CO2 hydrogenation selectivity of CeO2. We tested three samples at 300–400 °C and 1 bar. The vacancy-rich sample reached 92% methanol selectivity at 350 °C, compared with 78% for the pristine sample. The results suggest that oxygen vacancies may stabilize the formate intermediate, but the operando evidence remains indirect [1].
