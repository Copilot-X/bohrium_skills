# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — Silica Coating Reduces Capacity Fade in LiNiO2 Cathodes

C. Example, D. Sample

## Abstract

Silica coating is proposed to reduce capacity fade in LiNiO2 cathodes. We cycled coated and uncoated cells at 1 C between 2.8 and 4.3 V. After 100 cycles, the coated cell retained 88% capacity, compared with 71% for the uncoated cell. The result suggests that the coating may suppress surface reconstruction, but post-mortem evidence is limited.

## 2 Methods

LiNiO2 powder was coated with 1 wt% silica by sol-gel. Cells used a lithium metal anode and 1 M LiPF6 electrolyte. Cycling was performed at 25 °C and 1 C.

## 3 Results

The uncoated cell retained 71% capacity after 100 cycles. The coated cell retained 88% capacity under the same conditions. The initial discharge capacity was 210 mAh g−1 for both cells.

## 4 Discussion

The higher retention is consistent with suppressed surface reconstruction. However, the coating thickness was not measured after cycling, so the mechanism is not proven.
