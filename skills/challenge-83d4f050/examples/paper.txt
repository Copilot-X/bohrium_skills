Synthetic pathway source for the interface example. Not a formal answer.
In starved cells, ligand L1 bound receptor R1 at the plasma membrane.
L1 binding activated R1 and promoted recruitment of kinase K1 to the membrane.
Activated R1 activated K1 in the cytoplasm.
K1 phosphorylated kinase K2 at its activation loop.
Phosphorylated K2 phosphorylated transcription factor T1 in the cytoplasm.
Phosphorylated T1 was imported into the nucleus.
Nuclear T1 bound the G1 promoter and activated G1 transcription.
Phosphatase P1 dephosphorylated K2 and suppressed K2 activity.
Inhibitor I1 expression was associated with lower K1 activity.
An additional AKT branch was mentioned once but was not tested.
