## 1 Introduction

CO2 hydrogenation to methanol is an important carbon-utilization route. Ceria-based
catalysts can activate CO2 through surface defects. Previous studies reported that
oxygen vacancies increase CO2 conversion, but the effect on methanol selectivity is
less clear.

## 2 Results

CeO2 reached 12% conversion and 78% selectivity at 350 C. CeO2-x reached 18%
conversion and 92% selectivity at the same temperature.
