## Abstract

We studied CO2 hydrogenation over CeO2 at 350 C and 1 bar. The catalyst show a
high methanol selectivity of 92%, and it may be stable for 24 h. The result
suggest that oxygen vacancies play a important role.

## 1 Introduction

CO2 hydrogenation is a promising route for carbon utilization. However, the
relationship between oxygen vacancies and selectivity is not clear. Previous
studies reported that vacancy concentration correlates with conversion [1].
